Not every trip has to be a road trip. Some of the best days we run happen inside a single stretch of sand.

In July we set up a **Sip & Paint** on the Cotonou coast with our friends at Light Concepts — easels and canvases laid out under a thatched paillote, paint palettes on white tablecloths, the Atlantic doing its thing about fifty metres away. Everyone who booked said some version of the same sentence when they arrived: *I really can't draw.* Nobody left with a blank canvas.

![Easels and long tables set up under a thatched beach paillote before the Sip & Paint begins](/photos/sip-paint-beach-setup.jpg)

## How the day runs

You show up late morning to a seat with your name on it. The artist walks the group through the piece of the day — this time a figure study, though most people wandered off-brief within twenty minutes, which is the point. Somebody paints a camper van under palm trees. Somebody paints their partner. Somebody paints a woman in a headwrap and refuses to explain who she is.

![A guest painting a portrait in blue and yellow, complete with a wide-brimmed hat](/photos/sip-paint-hat-portrait-canvas.jpg)

The "sip" half is not an afterthought. Coconuts come straight off the tree and get opened in front of you, and there's grilled food and cold drinks running through the afternoon. People drift between their easel and the sand and back again.

![Two guests sipping fresh coconuts on the sand between painting sessions](/photos/sip-paint-coconut-friends.jpg)

## What actually makes it work

Three things, and none of them are the paint:

- **Nobody there can paint either.** The room levels itself within the first ten minutes.
- **It's a good day to come alone.** Half the group arrived solo. Nobody stayed solo — you end up leaning over to see what your neighbour is doing, and that's the whole social engine of the thing.
- **You leave holding something.** Not a photo on a phone, an actual object. It goes on a wall.

![Two guests holding up their finished paintings inside the MrUSB photo frames](/photos/sip-paint-frames-artwork-duo.jpg)

## Practical notes

- **Who it's for:** total beginners, couples, friend groups, solo travellers, teams. We've run it for all five.
- **What to wear:** something you don't mind a little paint on, and beach-appropriate. Aprons are provided but paint has opinions.
- **What's provided:** canvas, easel, paints, brushes, apron, a welcome drink and a coconut. You bring yourself.
- **How long:** roughly four to five hours, late morning into the afternoon, then photos on the sand.

![Friends holding photo frames and the colourful canvases they painted](/photos/sip-paint-frames-friends.jpg)
